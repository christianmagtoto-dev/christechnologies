How it connects

From one department to the entire business.

A customer order can affect Sales, Production, Inventory, Purchasing, Finance, and Delivery. CHRIS connects those processes, so when one department updates the system, the information flows to the teams that need it.

  1. Customer order
  2. Sales
  3. Production
  4. Inventory
  5. Purchasing (if materials are needed)
  6. Production
  7. Quality control
  8. Delivery
  9. Finance
  10. Management

Less duplication. Less manual coordination. Better visibility.

Automate the work behind your business

  • Order received: work order created
  • Materials required: purchasing notified
  • Production completed: inventory updated
  • Payment recorded: finance updated
  • Key activity: management dashboard updated
